## 4. The idea: Verity

**One line:** An open, explainable, pre-payment fraud tripwire for Medicare and Medicaid, built on public federal data, that finds ghost provider networks and impossible billing before the next check goes out, and hands a human investigator a ready-to-act packet.

**Why this specific thing, right now.** In the last 18 months the government did three things that make this buildable in 24 hours for the first time: (1) HHS released the **entire T-MSIS Medicaid provider spending file** (2018 to 2024, NPI by procedure code by month, 227M rows, 1.8M providers, ~$800B) as a public parquet in Feb 2026, and DOGE claimed it "would have easily detected" the Minnesota autism fraud; (2) CMS published **Hospice/HHA/SNF enrollment and All-Owners files, the Revoked Providers list, and the Market Saturation program-integrity dataset** with a moratorium flag; and (3) the White House built a task force whose 30/60/90-day mandate is exactly "find the vulnerabilities, set pre-payment standards, make states share data." Meanwhile Minnesota's Medicaid director publicly complained CMS "has not provided data or explanation on how the deferral amount was calculated." Nobody has shipped the transparent, reproducible, public version of the CMS war room. You will. Sources: [KFF on the T-MSIS release](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/what-newly-released-medicaid-data-do-and-dont-tell-us/), [OnHealthcare.tech](https://www.onhealthcare.tech/p/the-800b-open-secret-what-the-new), [CMS Medicaid Fraud War Room](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/cms-medicaid-fraud-war-room-stops-more-203-million-improper-payments-during-first-88-days), [Stateline on MN/CA deferrals](https://stateline.org/2026/07/21/citing-fraud-feds-halt-more-than-1b-in-medicaid-money-for-california-minnesota/), [MN Reformer](https://minnesotareformer.com/2025/12/18/u-s-attorney-fraud-likely-exceeds-9-billion-in-minnesota-run-medicaid-services/).

Medicare FFS $28.83B, Medicare Advantage $23.67B, Medicaid $37.39B (CMS, Jan 2026). OIG found 40 states paid $50.3M to 584 providers already terminated elsewhere. ### The three detectors (this is the technical depth)

**Detector 1: Ghost Networks (graph entity resolution).** Build a heterogeneous graph: providers (NPI), owners (from CMS All-Owners files), addresses, phone numbers, incorporation dates and states, managing employees, plus the Revoked Providers list, the OIG LEIE exclusion list, and SAM.gov exclusions. Resolve entities with a blocking-plus-embedding approach (normalize addresses and names, then a small sentence-embedding model for fuzzy owner and address matching, then a learned or rule-based match score). Then run connected components and community detection (Louvain or Leiden) and compute risk features per component: shared owners across multiple new hospices, incorporation bursts (many entities created within weeks of each other in the same county), addresses shared with revoked or excluded entities, ownership churn, providers per 10K beneficiaries in a county versus the CMS Market Saturation baseline. This is the mathematical core: the mathematicians own the scoring model and the statistical baselines; the developers own the pipeline. Output: ranked components with a "this cluster of 14 hospices shares 3 owners, 2 addresses, was incorporated over 40 days in Van Nuys, and one owner is on the LEIE" explanation. Datasets: Hospice Enrollments (`25704213-e833-4b8b-9dbc-58dd17149209`), Hospice All Owners (`e983965e-1603-4cb8-82b5-c40090e380d1`), HHA and SNF equivalents, Revoked Providers (`a6496a7d-4e19-479a-a9ad-d4c0a49e07c3`), Market Saturation (`8900b9c5-50b7-43de-9bdd-0d7113a8355e`), all via `https://data.cms.gov/data-api/v1/dataset/<id>/data`; LEIE CSV at `https://oig.hhs.gov/exclusions/downloadables/UPDATED.csv`; NPPES at `https://download.cms.gov/nppes/NPI_Files.html`.

**Detector 2: Impossible Days (the Medicaid parquet).** Take the T-MSIS Medicaid Provider Spending parquet (3.07 GB, at `https://stopendataprod.blob.core.windows.net/datasets/medicaid-provider-spending/2026-02-09/dataset/medicaid-provider-spending.parquet`; pre-filter to 3 to 5 states and time-based HCPCS codes with DuckDB or Polars before you do anything else). For every NPI-month, convert unit-based time codes (97153 ABA 15-minute units, H2015, T1019 personal care, 90837 psychotherapy, etc.) into implied clinician-hours per day. Flag providers whose implied hours exceed 24 per day per rendering clinician, whose beneficiary count grows faster than any plausible practice, or whose billing mix sits in the top 0.1% of a robust z-score against the state and specialty distribution. Show the Minnesota EIDBI/autism and housing-stabilization codes specifically, because that is the fraud the White House keeps citing and the state says CMS won't show its math. Your demo is the math, shown.

**Detector 3: Dead-in-Medicare, Alive-in-Medicaid.** Join the Medicare Revoked list and LEIE against the Medicaid Provider Enrollment Segments (T-MSIS provider views on opendata.hhs.gov) and the Medicaid spending file: providers revoked or excluded federally who still show Medicaid enrollment or paid claims afterward. That is the OIG $50.3M finding, reproduced live, and it is exactly what OBBBA forces every state to build by December 31, 2026. This detector is your commercial wedge (see below).

### The agent (Best Use of AI)

Not a chatbot. An investigator agent, built with the Codex credits, that takes a flagged cluster and (a) pulls every piece of evidence from the joined data, (b) drafts a CMS-style "Notice of Intent to Exclude" or payment-suspension referral packet with citations to 42 CFR 424.535 revocation grounds, (c) writes the plain-English explanation for a human reviewer, and (d) records the reviewer's accept/reject decision to retrain the risk score. That last part is Larson's "meaningful feedback loop" and Aslan's "scaling existing human judgment" in one feature. Use structured tool calls over your own data, not free-form generation, and show the evidence trail for every sentence the agent writes. Fanelli's translation-to-outcomes: the packet replaces a retrospective audit that today takes an analyst days.

### The UI (Best Design)

Build it in the **U.S. Web Design System** so it looks like it already lives on cms.gov. Three screens: a national map of risk clusters (county heat by dollars at risk), a cluster detail page with the ownership graph rendered and the evidence trail, and the "Monday packet" export. One more screen that will get you talked about: a **public "show the math" page** for a state, with the deferral dollar figure decomposed by detector, because that is the transparency the states are demanding and the administration should want to offer.

### The demo script (5 minutes)

Open cold with one sentence: "Medicaid and Medicare lost $66 billion to improper payments last year and the government just released the data to find it. We built Verity." Then: the map, one LA hospice cluster in the graph view (ownership links lit up, one owner on the exclusion list), one "impossible day" provider in Minnesota billing 31 hours a day, the agent producing the referral packet live, the reviewer clicking approve, the model updating. Close on three numbers: dollars flagged, providers flagged, hours saved per referral versus the manual process. Then the one slide the investors want: who buys this on Monday.

### Risks and how to pre-empt them
