# Verity demo script (three minutes)

Persona on screen: Maria Ortega, SIU lead at a Medicaid managed care plan. Every sentence below describes records; nothing asserts fraud.

1. **Landing (15 s).** "Integrity before the payment goes out." Verity screens every enrolled provider against fourteen public datasets and ranks referral candidates on an explicit evidence hierarchy. Scroll to the three numerals: tier 1 candidates, dollars paid after a screening trigger, ranked communities.
2. **Console (10 s).** Open the console. Press Cmd+K anywhere to search any provider in the country or jump to a page.
3. **Overview (20 s).** Tier KPIs, the county map (one figure per NPI, never summed across detectors), the top referral candidates and top communities.
4. **Candidates (25 s).** One row per provider: tier, detectors, score, dollars at risk from the tier-defining detector, reasons. Filter to tier 1. Point at a provider corroborated by two detectors.
5. **Provider page (40 s).** Registry record, billing history by program and year, and Procedures billed: the specific codes with their national comparison. Public list actions, service months after the action with the identity match tier and the file dates, implied hours by month with the rate-free lower bound, the communities the NPI belongs to. Click "Draft packet": Claude Opus 5 drafts from the evidence rows only; every finding cites row ids; grounds come from the 42 CFR mapping; caveats list what to rule out. Click "PDF".
6. **Ask this case (25 s).** Ask "What is the timeline of the action and the payments after it?" The answer cites the tool and row for every sentence and refuses anything outside the evidence tables.
7. **Network page (35 s).** Headline and numbered facts in plain language. The factor desk: thirty factors with percentile bars and a momentum reading (indicative). Then the graph: providers, owners, suites, phones; dashed outline means on a list.

7b. **Enforcement feed (20 s).** Filter Candidates to "Enforcement records." The spending file ends in December 2024; these rows come from Department of Justice releases, HHS-OIG enforcement records and state attorney general actions fetched daily and backfilled to January 2024, never news. Open one: "Adjudicated (pleaded guilty) per a Department of Justice release dated ..." with the record linked. A conviction is tier 1 because it is the record an OIG exclusion later cites; a charge is tier 3, documented and not adjudicated. On the network page, a named owner's conviction has propagated to every enrollment that person owns, months before the exclusion list will carry it.
8. **Reviewer decision (15 s).** Reject with a reason. The note is classified to the evidence family that was wrong and only that family's weight moves; the response says the weights stay informational until a few hundred reviews.
9. **Methods (15 s).** Generated by the code: every evaluation states its significance, including where labels overlap the score; identity match tiers and file dates for Detector 3; the denominator sensitivity for Detector 2.

Backup: if Supabase is unreachable the overview, candidates and landing read `web/public/fallback/*.json`.
