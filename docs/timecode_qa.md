## What was applied

- **Personal service set to Y for 26 codes** (0368T, 92507, 92508, 92526, 92609, 97110, 97113, 97116, 97129, 97130, 97158, 97530, 97533, 97535, 97537, 97750, 97755, 97760, 97761, 97763, G0153, G0159, G0160, G0161, G0162, H0004): constant-attendance therapy codes, speech-language pathology codes and clinician-delivered adaptive behavior codes are delivered by the qualified professional under the CPT descriptor. This makes the impossible-days test stricter for individual therapists; the concurrency rule (three or more billing organizations) and the single-organization tier B still apply, because therapy assistants commonly bill under a supervising therapist's NPI.
- **Untimed per-session codes keep their typical durations** (for example 90853 at 90 minutes, 92507 at 30 minutes). The descriptors define no time, so the table's minutes are typical durations used only to convert units into hours; they are labelled as assumptions in the table notes and the Methods page, and the rate-free lower bound does not depend on them.
- Every other disagreement is left for a human reviewer; none changes a tier A rule.
